# DECISIONS.md

Decisions taken for the Lenz Rescue rebuild, with the reason. If one of these is reversed, update the
entry rather than deleting it, and update the file it affects.

1. **Concept is Night Drive.** Amber equals the problem, cool white equals the solution, and the page
   gets visually clearer as you scroll. Chosen over an optical or a workshop direction because the
   product is literally light, and because it gives every colour and motion decision a rule to obey
   instead of a taste call. DESIGN.md section 1.

2. **The client's `#07170f` is kept.** It becomes `--pine` and carries the section bands and the
   scrolled header. A darker `--night` sits beneath it as the page ground so there is somewhere for
   depth to come from. We did not throw away the one brand decision they had already made.

3. **Six colours, three fonts, three radii, one spacing scale.** Hard limits written into DESIGN.md
   section 7 as a ban list. The failure mode on a build like this is not a bad choice, it is forty
   small unmanaged choices.

4. **The owner story is told once.** The current site tells it in two near-identical sections, "Our
   story" and "About", which reads as padding. It now appears once on the home page and once more at
   length on `/about`, and nowhere else. Tone shifts from "help a kid out" to "young tradesman who
   does the fourth step every time", because the apologetic version undercuts the price.

5. **The story is removed entirely from `/dealer-and-auction`.** A dealership buying twenty units
   wants turnaround and consistency, not charm. Different audience, different page, different voice.

6. **Single page becomes twelve routes, including four location pages.** The current site cannot rank
   for "headlight restoration Antioch" because there is no page about it. Location pages are written
   individually, not generated by city-name substitution, because near-duplicate location pages are
   worse than none.

7. **The Venmo icon and the unlinked Square icon come out of the footer.** Payment logos in a footer
   read as a market stall. Payment methods are stated in words on the FAQ instead, once the client
   confirms them. The dangling `#process` link and the empty-label "Z" icon go too.

8. **3D is one fragment shader, not a model.** The entire WebGL budget is a fullscreen plane running
   the beam shader in MOTION.md section 7. No GLTF, no loaders, no HDRI, no post-processing. A real
   3D headlight would need a model nobody has, would cost megabytes, and would look worse than the
   shader on a phone.

   **Status at handover: superseded by item 9, which was applied.** The shader stays in the
   repository as `src/components/hero/beam.frag` and is still the definition of the look. The
   poster, the share image and the haze layer are rendered from it at build time, and the live
   beam is drawn from the same figures. It is no longer run in the browser.

9. **Shader fallback, if the budget is missed.** If three.js pushes the home route past 230KB
   gzipped, replace it with a 2D canvas radial-gradient beam following the same lerped pointer. Do
   not substitute a different 3D library. The visual idea survives, the weight does not.

   **Status at handover: applied in Prompt 6.** With three.js and @react-three/fiber the home route
   measured 459.9KB of JavaScript gzipped, twice the budget. The beam is now a 2D canvas,
   `src/components/hero/BeamCanvas.tsx`, following the same pointer lerped at 0.06, with the touch
   drift, the pause when the hero leaves the viewport and the single static frame under reduced
   motion all kept. The home route now measures 224.4KB. `three`, `@react-three/fiber` and
   `@react-three/drei` are no longer dependencies. Files affected: MOTION.md sections 5.1, 7 and 9,
   each of which carries a note.

10. **Redirects to add in `next.config.ts`.** The old site is one page with anchors, so most of these
    are defensive against links already in the wild. All permanent.

    | From | To |
    |---|---|
    | `/index.html` | `/` |
    | `/assets/:path*` | `/media/:path*` |
    | `/#services` | `/headlight-restoration` |
    | `/#results` | `/gallery` |
    | `/#guide` | `/vehicle-guide` |
    | `/#about` | `/about` |
    | `/#contact` | `/contact` |
    | `/#request` | `/contact` |
    | `/#process` | `/headlight-restoration` |

    Anchor redirects cannot be done server side, since the fragment never reaches the server. Handle
    these with a small client-side check in the root layout that reads `location.hash` on first load
    and routes accordingly, then clears the hash.

11. **Reviews are built but not shipped empty.** The current site refers to reviews it does not show,
    which actively costs trust. The section exists in code behind a `ReviewPlaceholder` state and is
    kept out of the page order until the client supplies the Google profile URL, the rating, the
    count and three review texts. An empty reviews section is worse than no reviews section.

12. **The guarantee needs written terms before launch.** "3 year guarantee" appears three times on
    the current site with no terms anywhere. It now appears twice, with terms. An unqualified
    multi-year guarantee on a cosmetic restoration is a liability, not a selling point.

13. **Every image is treated, not shown raw.** The client's photos are phone photos taken in mixed
    light. The filter stack and the oxide and xenon tints in DESIGN.md section 5 are what make an
    inconsistent set read as a deliberate before and after system. Without that treatment the gallery
    will look like a Facebook album regardless of how good the rest of the site is.

14. **Placeholders ship visibly during the build.** `PlaceholderFrame` shows the expected filename
    and ratio, and `TODO(owner)` items render as small chips. The client sees exactly what is still
    missing at review time instead of discovering it at launch. Both are removed in Prompt 6.

    **Status at handover:** Prompt 6 removes each one once its real content exists, and none has
    arrived since the build began, so every frame and chip is still showing. `npm run audit:owner`
    lists them by route and section. The list as it stood at handover is in HANDOVER.md section 9
    and in full in `docs/qa/owner.txt`.

    **Reversed, 17 November 2026.** The build was put on a public staging address with the frames
    and the chips still in it, and visitors could read the working notes. Nothing unfinished is
    shown any more. The notes exist in development only: every one sits behind a check on
    `NODE_ENV`, and a production build carries none of them, on the page or in its script. A media
    slot with no file renders nothing, and the layout closes up around it. `PlaceholderFrame` is
    deleted and the chip is now `DevNote`. What is still owed is listed by `npm run audit:owner`
    from the source, and `npm run audit:seo` fails if a note, a filename or an empty frame reaches
    a built page.

15. **Reduced motion is a build requirement, not a nicety.** Spelled out per component in MOTION.md
    section 2 and checked in QA. A site whose whole concept is motion has to be usable without it.

16. **The beam is squashed, as its comment says, not stretched, as its code said.** MOTION.md section
    7 gave the line as `d.y *= 0.62` under the comment "squashed vertically so it reads as a lamp,
    not a dot". Multiplying the distance stretches the beam instead, to 1.6 times as tall as it is
    wide. Built that way through Prompts 2 to 5, it stood behind the transparent header and reached
    down onto the heading. Prompt 6 measured it, against the pixels actually behind each piece of
    text, with the beam at rest: the navigation links stood at 1.07 to 4.48 to 1 and the heading at
    1.00 to 2.44. On a phone, as the beam drifted, the wordmark, the menu button and the heading all
    fell to between 1.00 and 1.32, which is to say they disappeared. The line is now `d.y /= 0.62`,
    which is what the comment describes. Measured the same way: navigation 4.28 to 5.36, heading
    6.87 to 9.74, and on a phone the wordmark 8.21 and above, the menu button 4.37 and above and the
    heading 6.28 and above. It also reads as a lamp. It is one operator in three places:
    `src/components/hero/beam.frag`, the line that applies `SQUASH` in
    `src/components/hero/BeamCanvas.tsx`, and the same line in `scripts/beam-still.ts`. MOTION.md
    section 7 carries the corrected line and a note. To go back, turn the divide into a multiply in
    those three places, and expect `npm run audit:a11y` to fail on the hero.

17. **The beam gives way to what is read or pressed.** MOTION.md section 5.1 has the beam follow the
    pointer. Its centre is bright enough to wash out anything set over it, so whatever the pointer
    was on could not be read: the Call button vanished when it was hovered, and so did the
    navigation link being pointed at. MOTION.md section 8 keeps the phone number and the call to
    action usable at all times, and DESIGN.md section 2.3 holds text to its contrast. So over the
    header, the pitch, the figures and the scroll cue the beam now heads back to where it rests, at
    the lerp MOTION.md gives. It still follows the pointer over open ground and over the heading,
    which section 5.1 wants lit. No new value is involved. It is one list,
    `GIVES_WAY_TO` in `src/components/hero/HeroBackdrop.tsx`. Empty it to go back.

18. **Titles and descriptions come from SEO.md, and from nowhere else.** SEO.md section 4 gives every
    route its title and description and calls them written and final. They replace the four "Meta
    title" and "Meta description" lines in CONTENT.md, and the ones the build had made up for the
    pages CONTENT.md gave none. They are held in one file, `src/content/seo.ts`, with the keyword
    SEO.md section 3 gives each page, and a page passes only its path. Both `npm run audit:seo`
    and `npm run audit:copy` read the table out of SEO.md itself and fail if the site differs
    from it by a character, so SEO.md changes first. The city pages follow the pattern SEO.md
    writes out for Antioch.

19. **Twelve routes became fifteen.** Item 6 is superseded. SEO.md adds
    `/headlight-restoration-cost`, which it calls the most valuable page on the site, and
    `/tail-light-restoration` and `/marker-light-restoration`, on the fog light page's template.
    CONTENT.md has no copy for them. It was put together in the build, almost all of it from
    sentences CONTENT.md and SEO.md already contain, and it is in `docs/CONTENT-SEO.md` with the
    source of every line beside it and the few new lines marked NEW. It states no fact, price or
    promise those two files do not. The headings of the cost page are the questions SEO.md lists,
    and each answer is also what the page's FAQ data gives for that question.

20. **A page states a price only where it has one.** SEO.md section 7 asks for `minPrice: 125` on
    each service page. $125 is the price of a headlight booking, so it is stated of the service on
    the headlight page and the four city pages. The fog, tail and marker light pages are add ons
    with no price of their own yet. Their data gives the $125 as the price of the booking they are
    added to, through `isRelatedTo`, and states none for the add on, because a search engine or an
    assistant reading "fog light restoration, from $125" would be reading something nobody has
    said. The same two facts are on those pages in words, as two lines of the price list. The
    dealer page states no price at all: the trade price has not been supplied. `audit:seo` holds
    each kind of page to its rule.

21. **The share image is a real before and after pair.** SEO.md section 8 asks for the pair on it.
    `scripts/share.ts` draws the pair the home page slider leads with, side by side, with the
    slider's own seam between them, at 1200 by 630. The photos get the treatment item 13 gives
    every work photo, worked out on the pixels because a link preview has no stylesheet. It
    carries no lettering, so it uses no font. Its filename carries a hash of its contents, so a
    new picture has a new address and nothing goes on showing the old one. With no pair on disk
    it falls back to a frame of the beam.

22. **On a phone, the hero poster is the largest contentful paint.** SEO.md section 8 asks that the
    hero image be the LCP element and be preloaded. It was preloaded, and it was not the LCP
    element, because a browser takes a picture that fills the whole screen for a background and
    does not count it. The LCP element was the second line of the heading, which script reveals,
    and Lighthouse's default test scored the page 81 to 86 for it. The poster's box now stops one
    pixel short of the left edge of the screen, where the poster is darkest, and the preload asks
    for high priority. On every phone size measured the poster is now the LCP element, at 0.50s on
    slow 4G, and the default test scores 89 to 90. On a tablet or a desktop the browser still sets
    the poster aside, as a picture with too little detail in it, and the heading is the LCP
    element there. Nothing MOTION.md asks for was changed. It is `.hero-poster` in
    `src/styles/components.css`, and `npm run audit:perf` fails if the phone's LCP element is
    anything else.

23. **A figure that counts up is in the page once.** The Counter kept three copies of its figure in
    the document: one counting, one to hold the width and one for screen readers. A browser showed
    one. Anything reading the page as text, a search engine included, read `$125$125$125`. The
    figure is now one piece of text, and the other two are drawn by the stylesheet from an
    attribute, where they cannot be read as copy.

24. **A keyword is not written into an approved heading.** SEO.md section 5 wants each page's
    keyword in its H1 and once in the first 100 words of its body copy. Ten of the fifteen pages
    meet it. The three new pages were written to. The four city openings, which are the build's
    own drafts, each use theirs once: "headlight restoration in" and the city. The headlight page
    meets it because the sentence CONTENT.md section 4 wrote as that page's description, which
    SEO.md has now replaced as a description, leads the page instead. The other five are the home
    page, whose H1 SEO.md itself excuses, and the fog light page, the vehicle guide, the dealer
    page and the gallery. Their headings and body copy are CONTENT.md's, word for word, and rule 2
    of the build says copy is not rewritten. SEO.md says the same thing its own way: do not ruin a
    good headline for a keyword. `audit:seo` marks each of the five LOW and says which half of the
    rule it misses. HANDOVER.md section 11 lists them.
