# CONTENT-SEO.md

Copy for the three pages docs/SEO.md adds, and for the links that end a page.

CONTENT.md has no copy for these pages, so it was written in the build. Almost all of it is taken,
word for word, from sentences CONTENT.md and SEO.md already contain, and the source of each is given
beside it. The few lines that are new are marked NEW. Nothing here states a fact, a price or a
promise that those two files do not already state. It is for the owner to read and confirm, and when
it is approved it can move into CONTENT.md.

Section numbers carry on from CONTENT.md, which ends at 11. Titles and meta descriptions are not
here: they are in SEO.md section 4, which is final.
